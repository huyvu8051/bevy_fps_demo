footsteps/
    A per-surface footstep pack: each subdirectory holds its own license.txt
    naming the Freesound original it derives from. Licences are mixed, so
    check the subdirectory before switching a surface:

        gravel/  CC0 1.0          (public domain, no attribution needed)
        grass/   CC-BY 3.0        (attribution required: swuing / Freesound)

    gravel is used for the player and grass for zombies; see
    PLAYER_FOOTSTEP_SURFACE and ZOMBIE_FOOTSTEP_SURFACE in client/src/audio.rs.

    These are .ogg, which is why the client enables bevy's "vorbis" feature.
